Sources, limits & privacy

Read the feed, visit the publisher

Supports RSS 2.x, common RSS 1.0 and Atom feeds, plus OPML subscription lists. Only feed-provided titles and plain-text summaries are shown. No full-article scraping, images, embeds, audio downloads or tracking pixels. An external article opens only when you click its link.

Publisher dates and successful retrieval dates are separate. Missing publisher dates remain unknown. Duplicate links are grouped after removing fragments and common tracking parameters; distinct item IDs remain in your export. A grouped read/save action affects the visible group copies.

Manual refresh, clear limits

Refresh is on demand, sequentially. The public gateway may cache results for 15 minutes and has a shared daily allowance. Publisher blocks, deleted feeds and quota failures leave the last good items visible with an error. No automatic polling or refresh schedule runs.

Up to 50 feeds and 5,000 items per workspace. Each capture reads its first 300 entries; retained history keeps the newest 300 unsaved entries per feed plus saved items. Import/export is your backup, not a promise of permanent archiving. Files over 2 MB XML or 10 MB JSON are rejected.

What leaves this page

A public refresh sends the selected URL to the CoolCode gateway, which fetches the publisher. Do not use private or tokenized feeds there. Local XML and JSON/OPML imports stay in your browser. Clicking an article contacts that website. Device saves are deliberate and are not encrypted. The shared CoolCode counter records aggregate tool opens only.

References: RSS specification, Atom RFC 4287, OPML 2.0, NASA feeds. This reader is independent of the publishers.
